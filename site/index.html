      Payment reconciliation

      WAYA Bank · Data engineering

      Automated reconciliation across 33+ payment providers and bank statements, with a dispute portal and a consolidated warehouse.

      Manual reconciliation
      80% less work
      Dispute resolution
      48h → <24h
      How I built it

      The problem. Payment records across 33+ providers and bank statements needed matching, while operational data was spread across 50+ microservices.

      My contribution. I built the reconciliation system and dispute portal, and consolidated service data into a PostgreSQL warehouse with standardized schemas.

      The pipeline choice. Airflow handled batch loads; Kafka streamed high-priority wallet, NIP and withdrawal tables at under five minutes. Metabase dashboards and anomaly checks supported operational monitoring.

      The outcome. Manual reconciliation fell by 80%, and dispute resolution went from 48 hours to under 24. This is a summary of my professional work; the interactive example above uses synthetic data.

      Cloud cost reporting

      Freelance · Cloud FinOps

      Monthly cost reporting across 35+ AWS accounts, reconciling source exports to the reporting model and investigating anomalies.

      Reporting errors caught
      ~$135K
      Refresh and validation
      ~60% less time
      How I built it

      The problem. Monthly reporting across 35+ AWS accounts needed to reconcile billing exports with the Power BI model, without counting upfront charges twice.

      My contribution. I designed daily amortization, spreading upfront charges over the period they cover, and offset logic that removes the original lump sum from the same reporting total.

      The checks. I reconcile Cost Explorer exports to the reporting model and investigate anomalies. Separately, I caught about $135K of manual-refresh errors before leadership reporting.

      The delivery boundary. QuickSight dashboards are built; the pipeline from the master billing source is being developed with engineering. Refresh and validation take about 60% less time.
